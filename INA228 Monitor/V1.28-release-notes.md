## 1. R1 statement

### What V1.28 does

1. **Noise.**
   - `wifi: output_power: 11dB`
   - ADC_CONFIG `0xFDC5`: bus 2074 µs, shunt 4120 µs, temperature 50 µs,
     AVG 256
   - Both come from the diag2 run [M]:
     - TB-2: unlit sd 5.15 → 1.84 mA; ENERGY 0.449 → 0.231 W
     - TB-5: sd ratio 0.735
2. **SOC** = 100 + net / 397 Ah, where net is the INA228 CHARGE register since
   the anchor. No deadband.
   - O1: CE is applied to charge in.
   - `soc_offset_ma` (I_off, default 0) is subtracted.
   - The SD model is kept (inert at 0).
3. **The SOC ladder** (review B3). Never NAN.
   1. Confirmed anchor.
   2. Provisional anchor, seeded so SOC is continuous.
   3. SW-ledger movement from the last CHARGE SOC, while CHARGE is invalid.
4. **B2a:** SHUNT_CAL, ADC_CONFIG and TEMP_LIMIT are checked on every CHARGE
   poll. A runtime INA228 reset publishes nothing and reboots, so the boot
   check can bridge it.
5. **B2b:** the "first boot on sentinel firmware" branch is retired.
6. **B2c:** CHARGE counts only if it was read within 180 s.
7. **O2:** a full-charge anchor needs the CV current to have tapered to
   ≤ 10 A (`anchor_taper_max_a`).
8. **`max_current` 200 → 400 A.** One substitution now drives the driver, the
   CHARGE/ENERGY conversion and PWR_LIMIT (0x0960). The first boot rescales the
   bridge offsets, so HW Net Charge and HW Energy continue without a step.
9. **New entities:**
   - SOC Source
   - SOC (SW Ledger)
   - Ah In / Ah Out / Wh In / Wh Out Since Anchor
   - Hours Since Anchor
   - Mean Net Current Since Anchor
   - Last Anchor Closure
   - Suggested Offset Current
   - Last Charge Session Ah / Wh (CHARGE)
   - INA228 Noise Gauge and Noise State
   - INA228 Config Readback
   - WiFi Power Save (Driver)
   - WiFi TX Power (Driver)
10. **RECON.**
    - Last Unseen Drain, Recommended SD Rate and Suggested Offset Current now
      come from CHARGE brackets between two clean full charges.
    - The V1.27 ledger RECON is still logged, no longer published.

### Review fixes (2026-09-25, still 1.28: never flashed)

1. **O2 straddle** (c88d972). A CV reading counts only once the next 5 s
   reading is also CV. The one 1.6 s-averaged reading that straddles a mid-CV
   charger stop reads between I_cv and 0, and Absorption Phase is still held on
   by its 30 s `delayed_off`. Committed directly, a stop at 30 A read 8 A and
   anchored a false 100 % [M: host S4c].
2. **B2a reboot rate limit** (4bc9504). A chip that keeps resetting rebooted the
   node about once a minute. Now, after 3 reset reboots in a row, the next waits
   for 30 min of uptime, and the run clears after 1 h with the config held.
   Replaying 3 h of one reset a minute gives 8 reboots, not 180 [M: host S2b].
   Meanwhile CHARGE is not used, and Battery Current reads 1.09× (SHUNT_CAL
   4096 / 3750 [D]). 3 / 30 / 60 are design choices, not measurements.
   `is_running()` stops a `component.update` inside the 2 s delay (charger
   stop, Mark as Fully Charged) counting as a second reset.
3. **Hours Since Anchor carried across a reboot** (54f060e). It read 0 after a
   reboot until HA time came back, and an anchor set before HA time was valid
   restarted its hours from 0 at the first valid time after a reboot. Now it is
   saved in 0.1 h steps, runs on from the saved value plus uptime until HA time
   is valid, and a late-seeded epoch includes the carried hours [M: host S10].
   No effect while `soc_offset_ma` = 0, the default.

Restored globals: 45 → 47 (`ina_reset_reboots`, `hours_since_anchor_saved`).

### Decisions (turnover §5)

1. **While the anchor is NAN** (now), SOC runs on a **provisional anchor
   seeded from the SW ledger**.
   - SOC Source says so, so the SOC is never silently wrong (R8).
   - There is no step at the flash.
   - The ledger's own error (~3.3 %, turnover §2) stays in the SOC until the
     next full charge. That charge measures it (§3 below).
2. **Not seeded from the ~96.7 % estimate.** Only an absorption-confirmed,
   tapered full charge makes a confirmed anchor.
3. **Staleness:** SOC updates on every CHARGE read (`on_value`), not on a
   separate poll.
4. **Version strings:** header, `project.version` and the boot log all say
   1.28.

### At the first full recharge

1. The log shows the CV tail.
   - It should be ≤ 10 A (LiTime stops at 6.48 A).
   - SOC Source → "anchored at a full charge"; SOC = 100 %.
2. **Last Charge Session Ah (CHARGE)** ≈ (13.1 + 0.2 × d₁ + 0.25 × d₂) / 0.99 Ah.
   - 13.1 Ah is the drain the ledger had missed by 09-24 [D, turnover §2,
     ±4.4 Ah from the INA228 offset bound].
   - d₁ is days from 09-24 to the flash, at ~0.2 Ah/day [M: 8–9 mA, ledger
     books 0].
   - d₂ is days from the flash to the recharge, at ~0.25 Ah/day [D: quiet
     state].
3. **Last Anchor Closure** ≈ **+(13.1 + 0.2 × d₁) Ah ± 4.4 Ah.** This is the
   ledger's error, carried in through the provisional anchor.
   - **This is the check Bill asked for.**
   - Inside that band: the monitor's drain accounting holds, and the
     turnover's ~96.7 % estimate was right.
   - Well above it (> ~+18 Ah): the bank lost charge the shunt did not see,
     and B1 reopens.
4. The RECON log says "not measured", because the bracket started
   provisionally. The next clean full charge, ≥ 14 d later, measures it.

### At the second clean full charge (≥ 14 d after the first)

- RECON (CHARGE) publishes U and Suggested Offset Current.
- **Expected:** |U| within 2σ, and an offset near 0. At 20 d, σ ≈ 1.3 Ah is
  set by the INA228 offset limit [D: host S5].
- Set `soc_offset_ma` only if U is outside 2σ.

## 4. Flash notes

- **OTA only.** USB must not be connected while the 12 V bus is live (wiring
  summary §4).
- **Logs:** `logger: baud_rate: 0`, so read them with `esphome logs` or the HA
  ESPHome device log, not over USB serial.
- **Rollback to V1.27** (the fallback build, `0x6b05d980`):
  - SOC is safe, because V1.27's SOC is the ledger.
  - V1.27 has no LSB migration. It reads the register at 200 A, so **HW Net
    Charge and HW Energy will step once.**
  - If V1.28 goes back on after that, it cannot tell which counts V1.27 made.
    `hw_lsb_a` already reads 400 A, so it does not migrate.
    - Charge that moved while V1.27 ran is read at twice its size.
    - That is ~0.25 Ah, or 0.06 %, per day of rollback at idle [D: 10.5 mA x
      24 h / 397 Ah].
    - The next full charge re-anchors it away.
